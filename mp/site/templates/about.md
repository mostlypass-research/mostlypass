## About

{{FOUNDER}}

Most betting content sells certainty. This project publishes the opposite: what a betting price actually implies, where the bookmaker's margin sits, where a transparent model disagrees with the market, and why most games are not worth betting.

**What we publish.** A daily NFL research card that grades every game BET, LEAN or PASS against the sharpest available market. It includes fair prices, threshold prices and the exchange price where one exists. Every signal is timestamped before kickoff and graded automatically against the closing line.

**What we've learned so far.** Our first model was tested against thousands of NFL closing lines and did not beat them. So it is not allowed to call anything a bet. Until a model earns that out of sample, everything here is research and paper signals.

**What we don't do.** No locks. No guarantees. No screenshots. No deleted losses. No affiliate links. No selling picks.

The author publishes under the project name. The record is the credential.
